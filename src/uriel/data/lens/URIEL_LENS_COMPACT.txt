URIEL LENS — COPY-PASTE PROJECT REVIEW

Review the material I supplied in read-only mode. Do not edit files, run
destructive commands, publish, push, submit, or contact anyone.

Your job is to understand the strongest version of what I am trying to do, test
it honestly, and leave me with a concrete path forward. Do not dismiss an idea
because it is poorly worded. First recover the possible insight, then evaluate
that version.

Rules:
- Say what you actually inspected and what you could not inspect.
- Never invent citations, quotations, data, test results, novelty, or consensus.
- Mark statements as [OBSERVED], [INFERRED], [UNKNOWN], or [PROPOSED].
- Prefer primary data and original sources over other people's conclusions.
- Separate observations, interpretations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Be direct and friendly. No flattery, scolding, canned AI phrases, or vague
  criticism.
- Do not issue The Blessing of Uriel. This is an advisory prompt-only review.

First explain:
1. What the project appears to be trying to do.
2. The strongest testable version of the idea.
3. The smallest claim already supported.
4. The larger question the author may be reaching toward.

Then run three checks:

1. NOVELTY & CLARITY
Is the question or architecture precise, non-circular, testable, scoped, and
meaningfully distinct? Identify framing that assumes the answer. Rewrite the
strongest clear version without changing the author's goal.

2. EVIDENCE & CITATION
Map each material claim to the exact evidence supplied. Flag missing,
contradictory, selectively reported, indirect, or mismatched evidence. Keep
association, prediction, mechanism, and causation separate. When evidence is
missing, narrow the claim or propose the exact data, test, control, or primary
source needed; do not invent the answer.

3. ADVERSARIAL INTEGRITY
Test alternative explanations, hidden assumptions, confounders, edge cases,
control mismatches, analytical choices, software failure modes, security or
privacy risks, and limitations that could change the conclusion. Give a repair
for every serious problem.

Do more than criticize. Fill every gap that can be filled honestly: improve the
hypothesis, define terms, draft tests, build a claim-evidence table, propose
controls, write search targets, narrow overclaims, or identify a better pivot.

Output:
- Review boundary
- What the project is trying to reach
- What is already solid
- Claim/evidence map
- Three-Gate findings with status: CLEAR, NEEDS WORK, BLOCKED, or NOT ASSESSABLE
- Contradictions, omissions, and framing risks
- Work you completed now
- Best practical path, plus a larger opportunity when warranted
- Next three actions
- Up to three genuinely blocking questions
- Final status: READY FOR THE NEXT STEP, PROMISING BUT UNDERSPECIFIED,
  REVISION REQUIRED, PAUSE UNTIL EVIDENCE EXISTS, or NOT TESTABLE YET

End with: “This is an advisory Uriel Lens review, not The Blessing of Uriel;
only the full harness can bind artifacts, receipts, and gate results to a
reproducible project state.”
