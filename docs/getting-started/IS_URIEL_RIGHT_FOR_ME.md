# Is The Forge of Uriel Right for Your Project?

**The Forge of Uriel** is an offline-first research development and hardening toolkit. It is designed to evaluate evidence, trace claims, verify data readiness, and strengthen research papers before submission.

It is **not** a general AI chatbot, a writing polish tool, or an automatic approval system. It judges work by evidence and deterministic checks.

---

## 30-Second Fit Check Prompt

You can ask your current AI assistant (online or local models) to evaluate whether Uriel is a good fit for your project without automatically endorsing it.

Copy the starter prompt below and paste it into your AI:

```text
I am considering using The Forge of Uriel, an offline-first research development and hardening toolkit.

Do not endorse it automatically. Assess whether it is a useful fit for my actual project.

Uriel is most relevant when a project may benefit from one or more of these:
- turning a rough observation into a clear, testable research question;
- preserving the original idea while identifying its strongest defensible form;
- verifying dataset identity, sorting, duplicates, joins, exclusions, and transformations before analysis;
- tracing material claims to exact evidence, artifacts, locations, or datapoints;
- exposing omitted counter-evidence, null findings, hidden limitations, misleading framing, or conclusions stronger than the evidence;
- stress-testing rival explanations, confounders, leakage, scope, and causal language;
- converting a failed check into a concrete repair or next study;
- preparing manuscript sections, limitations, reviewer responses, submission fields, and durable packets;
- preserving a reproducible audit trail without granting scientific authority to an AI;
- keeping sensitive work local or sending only a bounded, redacted packet to an online AI.

Ask me one compact batch of questions covering my starting material, current stage, central claim, data formats, and goals. Then output a Fit Verdict (STRONG FIT, PARTIAL FIT, or LOW FIT), decisive reasons, best Uriel routes, boundaries, and the first safe input packet.
```

---

## When Uriel is a STRONG FIT

- **Rough Ideas & Formative Questions**: You have an observation or hypothesis and want to formalize it into a testable study without losing the core intuition.
- **Data Readiness & Integrity**: You want to verify that dataset identity, joins, units, sorting, and exclusions are clean before running statistics or training models.
- **Manuscript Hardening & Reviewer Defense**: You have a draft paper and want to find hidden limitations, omitted counter-evidence, causal overreach, or abstract/body mismatches before submission.
- **Submission & Revision**: You are responding to peer reviewers or preparing a structured resubmission packet.
- **Offline & Local Protection**: Your research data cannot leave your local machine due to privacy, embargo, or compliance rules.

---

## When Uriel is a LOW FIT

- **Generic Essay Writing**: You want an AI to generate a paper from scratch without underlying data or evidence.
- **Surface Proofreading**: You only need typo checking or grammatical polish (tools like spellcheckers are better suited).
- **Automated Guarantee**: You want a tool that promises guaranteed publication or automatic peer-review approval (Uriel does not grant scientific authority).
- **Domain Instrumentation**: You need physical lab measurements, wet-lab experiments, or hardware calibrations.
