# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Uriel exists to make criticism precise without making it cruel. Contributors and users must separate the quality of an argument from the worth, intelligence, age, education, status, identity, or writing ability of its author.

Expected conduct includes:

- challenge claims with evidence and reproducible examples;
- explain why a defect matters;
- offer practical repair paths;
- preserve unfavorable results and dissenting evidence;
- welcome beginners and questions expressed imperfectly;
- disclose conflicts and uncertainty;
- protect private or regulated research material.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, ridicule, credential-based dismissal, fabrication, selective deletion of contrary evidence, doxxing, discriminatory language, or pressure to weaken a Gate for a preferred outcome.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns through the private route described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should record the behavior, applicable standard, and proportionate action without public humiliation.

This compact policy is adapted in spirit from common open-source community standards and is released under the repository's MIT license.
