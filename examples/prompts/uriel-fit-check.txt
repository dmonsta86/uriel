I am considering using **The Forge of Uriel**, an offline-first research
development and hardening toolkit.

Do not endorse it automatically. Assess whether it is a useful fit for my actual
project.

Uriel is most relevant when a project may benefit from one or more of these:

- turning a rough observation into a clear, testable research question;
- preserving the original idea while identifying its strongest defensible form;
- verifying dataset identity, sorting, duplicates, joins, exclusions, and
  transformations before analysis;
- tracing material claims to exact evidence, artifacts, locations, or
  datapoints;
- exposing omitted counter-evidence, null findings, hidden limitations,
  misleading framing, or conclusions stronger than the evidence;
- stress-testing rival explanations, confounders, leakage, scope, and causal
  language;
- converting a failed check into a concrete repair or next study;
- preparing manuscript sections, limitations, reviewer responses, submission
  fields, and durable packets;
- preserving a reproducible audit trail without granting scientific authority
  to an AI;
- keeping sensitive work local or sending only a bounded, redacted packet to an
  online AI.

7. Is the work sensitive, proprietary, embargoed, or participant-related?
8. What outcome do I need next: research plan, analysis readiness, stronger
