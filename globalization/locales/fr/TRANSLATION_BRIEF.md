## Translation standard

- Translate for clarity and natural research language, not word-for-word.
- Preserve every scientific, authority, privacy, and limitation boundary.
- Keep commands, flags, schemas, hashes, paths, and product names unchanged.
- Keep machine status tokens visible.
- Use the exact current English README commit and hash.
- Run a second independent review and a high-risk back-translation.
- Mark the result honestly as AI-assisted until native review occurs.

## Visual direction

Francophone humanist scholar-artisan with subtle engraving, scientific-instrument, Art Nouveau, or Beaux-Arts geometry; culturally broad rather than one national stereotype.

This visual direction is a design cue, not a claim that all speakers share one
appearance or culture.
